# The first three seconds

Treat the opening as a separate edit. It answers the reason-to-stay test: "In the first 3 seconds is someone convinced they need to watch until the end?"

## Build three openings

Build three materially different rough openings from the same truthful source speech: for example problem first, result glimpse, and recognition first. Compare their first three seconds for topic clarity, curiosity, muted comprehension, and follow-through. Record the choice and the reason. Choose on the reason to stay, not on the loudest effect.

For each opening record:

- the viewer benefit;
- the unresolved question;
- the visible evidence by second three;
- the exact payoff scene.

Make every promise one this footage fulfils, and make it specific. An advertisement may resolve a concrete example or explain a mechanism before it invites the viewer to learn more. If it promises a tutorial, deliver the tutorial, not only a registration CTA.

## The image

Make the first frame meaningful on its own: a surprising action, a contradiction, a result glimpse, or a revealing human expression. Use an impact or transition only when it sharpens that idea; a quiet, impossible-looking result can beat a loud montage. Vary the device from reel to reel instead of a fixed stamp, flash, or 0.3 s effect.

The opening works muted. Establish the topic or tension at once, then reveal another meaningful state before second three. Start on the argument: no unrelated logo bumper, added silence, or repeated flashes. Match the chosen material style.

## The sound

Use a supplied sound reference when there is one. Remove the effect's recording lead-in and align its audible impact with the visual landing within two frames. Shape its level and tail around the first words, so the hit is noticeable and the first spoken word stays clear; briefly dip the music if that helps. Loudness supports the hook; it is never the hook.

## Check

Save a first-three-seconds shot and sound plan. Inspect the encoded opening frame by frame: the first frame, the impact, the reveal, and the return to the main edit. Confirm the first spoken word survives the impact mix. Record the result as its own opening-hook gate in `VERIFY.md`.
