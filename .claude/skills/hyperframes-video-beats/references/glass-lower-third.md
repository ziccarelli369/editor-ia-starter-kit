# Glass lower third

Read this when a topic span needs a glass card. A glass lower third supports the current idea while the speaker stays visible.

## When to use it

- Explanation scaffolds
- Topic labels
- Equation-style mental models
- Small lists
- "What this means" translations
- Pacing fills during long talking-head stretches
- A follow-up card after a shortened full-screen or split-screen beat

## Durations

- Explanation: 12-30 seconds.
- Long topic: 25-45 seconds, with staggered sub-elements that build as the narration evolves.

## Placement and style

- Bottom-center by preference, inside the lower third or lower half, clear of the speaker's face.
- Width about 68-72vw, max about 1240-1280px. Keep cards compact.
- Reuse `.glass-card dim lower-third` or the closest existing class: the dark frosted style the project already uses.
- Use the equation and list helpers when the idea is structural.
- Content: one headline plus an optional short subline, or a 2-4 item list.
- One card per topic span; a card that flashes per sentence breaks the topic.
