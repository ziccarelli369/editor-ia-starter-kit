# Paper takeover: full-screen crumpled paper

Read this when a topic span needs a paper beat. A paper takeover briefly makes one idea the whole frame.

## When to use it

- Hook payoff or "in this video" overview
- Major section transition
- Big stat or study result
- Memorable thesis line
- Short quote or mantra
- A contrast the audience should stop and look at

Paper is occasional punctuation. Long explanations get a glass lower third, which keeps the speaker visible.

## Durations

- Overview: 8-10 seconds.
- Stat: 5-8 seconds.
- Any paper beat: usually 4-12 seconds.

## Style

- Serious, professional typography with restrained font weight.
- Sparse black text: kickers, labels, arrows, sources, quotes and supporting text are all black.
- Accent blue only on a true stat number or one rare power word.
- Calm full-screen type: playful, huge or over-bold type only when the user asks for it.
- Deterministic crumpled-paper motion.

## Copy pattern

```text
In this video
-> The study
-> The stats
-> The paths you can take
-> What actually matters
```
